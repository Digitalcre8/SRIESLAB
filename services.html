01Company narrative

We meet with you and your team to understand your science or technology, the market, your regulatory route, financial plan and timelines. From this information and our own research, we develop one narrative that your pitch deck, website and LinkedIn are built from. We find out who you’re targeting, such as a specialist biotech VC, a generalist fund, a family office or a pharma partnering team, and develop messages for those specific audiences.

What’s included
  • Initial session with you and your team
  • First draft of the narrative and key messages (the three or four points investors and partners should remember)
  • Second meeting to go through the draft and gather feedback
  • Final narrative, usually around one page, and key messages

TimingTwo to three weeks

02Pitch deck and one-pager

What’s included
  • Investor deck of 10 to 15 slides, with a non-confidential version to send before an NDA
  • One-page summary

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can we start with a single service?

Yes. Most founders begin with the narrative, because the deck, website and LinkedIn are all easier to write once it’s agreed. If your website or LinkedIn is the more urgent problem, we’ll start there.

Can you work with my existing deck or website?

Yes. Many founders come to us with a pitch deck, website and LinkedIn profiles already in place. We review them first and tell you what’s working, what isn’t, and whether each one needs rebuilding or a few changes. Sometimes it’s the content of the slides that needs work, and sometimes it’s the design.

Our science can be hard to explain to non-specialists. Can you make it clear to investors without losing accuracy?

Yes. We keep the technical detail and data an investor needs and move the rest to an appendix. Specialist scientific writers keep the content accurate, and a medical illustrator helps visualise your mechanism of action, technology or clinical workflow.

Do you guarantee we’ll raise?

No. Whether you raise depends on your data, your team, the market and the timing. What we can guarantee is the quality of your materials, that they’re ready before your meetings, and that we keep you visible to investors while you raise.

How long does a project take?

Raise Ready takes three to four weeks, a Story Sprint four weeks and Launch 10 to 12 weeks. We’ll tell you what’s realistic for where you are in your raise before you commit to anything.

If you’ve already started or plan to raise in the next 12 to 18 months, book a 30-minute call with us

We’ll talk through where you are in your raise and what you already have in place, and work out your next steps.
