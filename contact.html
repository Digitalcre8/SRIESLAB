FAQ

Common questions

How do you price packages?

Tell us your stage and what you already have, and we'll quote for what you need. Packages always cost less than buying the same services separately.

Can I buy a single service?

Yes. Any service can be bought on its own, or added to a package or ongoing support.

Do you do medical illustration?

Yes, quoted per project. We also work with a network of independent specialists in other disciplines.

How does RAISE LAB payment work?

£5,000 at the start (or £1,750 a month for three months), then £5,000 when your next round closes or 12 months after the programme, whichever comes first.
