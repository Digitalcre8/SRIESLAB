FAQ

Common questions

How do you price packages?

The quote depends on your stage and what you already have in place, so we agree it after a 30-minute call. A package always costs less than buying the same services separately. If your pitch deck or website only needs a few changes, we’ll quote for those changes only.

Can I buy a single service?

Yes. You can buy any service on its own, or add it to a package or to ongoing support. Most founders start with the narrative, because the deck, website and LinkedIn are easier to write once it’s agreed.

What types of companies do you work with?

Biotech, medtech and deep tech companies, from pre-seed to Series A. Most of our clients so far have been therapeutics companies, and we’ve also worked with medical device companies. The questions investors ask are different for a drug, a device or a platform, and we plan the narrative around them.

Do you guarantee we’ll raise?

No. Whether you raise depends on your data, your team, the market and the timing. We take responsibility for the quality of your materials and for how visible you are to investors while you’re raising.

We’re preparing for a licensing deal, not a funding round. Can you help?

Yes. A pharma or medtech partnering team expects different information from a VC. We start by asking who you’re approaching, then plan the narrative and materials for them.

Do you do medical illustration?

Yes. We work with a medical illustrator who can draw your mechanism of action, technology or clinical workflow. It’s quoted per project.

SRIES Lab works with biotech, medtech and deep tech founders and CEOs preparing to raise, from pre-seed to Series A.
