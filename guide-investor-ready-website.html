The investor-ready website checklist

What an early-stage life science website needs before you start a raise, page by page.

Contents
  1. What the website is for
  2. Home
  3. Science or technology
  4. Pipeline or programmes
  5. Team
  6. News
  7. Contact
  8. Before you launch

01What the website is for

Investors often look at your website before they open your deck. It doesn't need to be large. It needs to confirm that you are credible and that your story holds together. Six pages is usually enough.

02Home

  • A headline that matches the opening of your investor narrative
  • One or two sentences on the problem and your approach
  • A clear route to the science, team and contact pages

03Science or technology

  • An explanation a non-specialist can follow
  • One clear visual of how your approach works
  • Links to publications or posters, if you have them

04Pipeline or programmes

  • Each programme, its indication and its current stage
  • Stages that match what your deck says

05Team

  • Photos and short bios for founders and leadership
  • Board members and scientific advisers
  • Links to LinkedIn profiles

06News

  • Recent milestones, grants, publications and appointments
  • Dates on every item, and nothing that looks abandoned

07Contact

  • A clear way to reach the company
  • A named contact for investors and partners, if you can

08Before you launch

  • Headline messages match your deck and LinkedIn
  • Pages work well on a phone
  • Page titles and descriptions are filled in for search
  • Every link works
