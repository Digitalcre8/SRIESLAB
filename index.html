Services

Four ways we help founders raise

01Science and investor story

We start with a session with your founder and CSO, then run a workshop to work out what makes the science worth investing in. You get positioning, key messages and a written investor narrative.

  • Positioning and key messages
  • Written investor narrative
  • A story and deck refresh when you raise again
Talk to us about this →

02Pitch deck and one-pager

A 15-slide pitch deck and a one-page summary, written and designed around the agreed story. Two rounds of revisions are included.

  • 15-slide investor deck
  • One-page summary
  • Data room pack and technical appendix for diligence
Talk to us about this →

03Brand and website

A logo, colour palette, typography and guidelines, and an investor-ready website of up to six pages that we design, write, build and launch. If you already have a site, we audit it and redesign it around your story.

  • Brand identity and guidelines
  • New website, up to six pages
  • Website audit and redesign
Talk to us about this →

04LinkedIn and investor preparation

Investors look you up before they reply. We audit and rewrite founder, leadership and company profiles, and get you ready for meetings with pitch pressure-testing, mock investor sessions and Q&A preparation with a specialist life sciences adviser.

  • Founder, team and company profiles
  • Mock investor sessions with written feedback
  • Preparation and debrief around real investor meetings

Seed · 8 to 10 weeks

Raise Ready

For seed companies with a deck, brand and website that need sharpening before the next raise.

  • Story and deck review, sharpened narrative, updated deck and one-pager
  • Website audit, redesign and rewrite
  • Pitch pressure-testing, two mock investor sessions and Q&A preparation

Approaching Series A · 3 to 4 months

Series A Readiness

For companies whose story and materials need to match a Series A.

  • A new story built from interviews with your leadership team
  • New 15-slide deck, one-pager and website redesign
  • Three mock investor sessions and Q&A preparation
  • LinkedIn for you and two of your leadership team
  • Data room pack and technical appendix
  • Preparation and debrief for two real investor meetings
