Visibility

Why we talk about visibility

We talk about visibility a lot, and we know it can sound a bit vague. It comes from years in marketing and communications, and from watching which companies raise. Investors often prioritise companies they’ve already heard of, through their own networks or in the news. So we help you show up regularly, online, at events and in industry media, across the 12 to 18 months of a raise. Each appearance reminds investors and partners who you are, your team’s expertise and proposition, so you remain a priority over time.

How we work

How we work with you

  1. 01
    A first session with you and your team

    We use this session to understand your science or product and the data behind it, your target market and competitors, your regulatory route, milestones and funding plan, and the investors or partners you’re approaching.

  2. 02
    Your narrative

    We draft your narrative: what you’re developing, why it matters, why now and which milestone this round gets you to. Then we draw out your key messages, the three or four points every investor should remember, and adapt them for each audience you’re meeting, such as VCs, family offices or pharma BD. Together they become the brief for your pitch deck, website and LinkedIn.

  3. 03
    Your materials

    We create your pitch deck and one-page summary from the narrative, plus your brand, website and LinkedIn if they’re in your package.

Our promise

What we can and can’t promise

We can’t promise you’ll raise. That depends on your data, your team, the market and the timing.

But we can promise that you’ll go into those meetings with a clear story and a pitch deck that makes the case, and that everything investors find afterwards, on your website and on LinkedIn, reinforces that story and your conviction in what you’re building.
