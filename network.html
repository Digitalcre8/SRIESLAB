Work with us as an independent specialist

We bring independent specialists into projects for early-stage biotech, medtech and deep tech companies that are preparing to raise. If you have experience working with life science companies within marketing, design, investor relations or financial advisory, we’d love to hear from you.

How it works

Introduce yourself

Our clients are founders and CEOs of companies between pre-seed and Series A. Most are scientists or engineers, and most are raising while running the company.

We engage specialists project by project. Before you start, we agree the brief, the timeline and your fee.

Projects last from four weeks to six months and cover the narrative, pitch deck, brand, website and LinkedIn. You’d join for the part that needs your discipline.
