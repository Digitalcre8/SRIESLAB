Ongoing support

Monthly support while you raise

If a fixed project doesn’t suit your stage, timeline or budget, we can work with you month by month instead, or continue to support you once a project ends. We can keep your LinkedIn, pitch deck and website up to date, and help with design and announcements as they come up.

Each month

What we can do each month

We agree the focus with you at the start of each month, from these four areas.

Updates

Pitch deck and website updates

When you have new data, a new hire or a change to the round, we update the pitch deck, one-page summary and website together, so they keep reinforcing the same story.

Design

Design and branding

We take on one-off design work as you need it, such as a conference poster, a figure or slides for a board meeting, in your existing brand.

Comms

Media announcements

We plan and write your company announcements, such as trial results, a new board member or a funding close, and regular updates to the investors you’re already talking to. We have communications and PR expertise.

Content

LinkedIn posts and content

We plan and write posts for your profile and the company page. In a quiet month that might be your view on a new paper, or what you heard at a conference, so investors still see your name between milestones.

FAQ

Questions about ongoing support

Is there a minimum commitment?

Three months. After that, support continues month to month and you can stop with a month’s notice.

Can the focus change from month to month?

Yes. One month might be mostly LinkedIn content, and the next might be a deck update ahead of a board meeting. We agree it with you at the start of each month.

How much does it cost?

It depends on what you need each month. Book a 30-minute call, tell us what you have in mind and we’ll send a quote.

Can I add a single service?

Yes. Any service can be bought on its own or added to a package or to ongoing support.

Do I need to have done a project with you first?

No. You can start with monthly support, or move to it after a project.

If you’ve already started or plan to raise in the next 12 to 18 months, book a 30-minute call with us

We’ll talk through where you are in your raise and what you already have in place, and work out your next steps.
