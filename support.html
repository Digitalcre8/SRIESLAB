FAQ

Questions about ongoing support

What is the minimum commitment?

Three months. After that, support continues month to month.

Can the focus change from month to month?

Yes. Support is bespoke, so we agree what to work on with you as your needs change.

How much does it cost?

It depends on what you need each month. Talk to us and we'll put together a quote.

Can I add a single service as well?

Yes. Any service can be bought on its own, or added to a package or ongoing support.
