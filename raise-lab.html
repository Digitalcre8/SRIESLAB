FAQ

Before you apply

Who is RAISE LAB for?

Pre-seed life science founders preparing a first raise who want to work alongside other founders.

How do I apply?

Use the contact form and choose RAISE LAB application. Tell us about your science and where you are with your raise.

How big are the groups?

Four to six founders, so everyone gets time in group sessions. You also get six hours of one-to-one support.

When is the second payment due?

When your next round closes or 12 months after the programme, whichever comes first.

Can I spread the first payment?

Yes. Pay £5,000 at the start, or £1,750 a month for three months.

I'd rather work one to one.

Have a look at the Launch package, which covers the story, deck, brand and website built just for you.
