Our studio

How we work with founders

Who you will work with, how a project runs, and the specialists we bring in when a project needs them.

Every project starts with your founder and CSO in the room

  • Find the story

    A session with your founder and CSO, then a workshop on what makes the science investable.

  • Write it down

    Positioning, key messages and the investor narrative, agreed with you before anything is designed.

  • Build the materials

    Deck, one-pager, brand, website and LinkedIn, all written from the same story.

  • Practise

    Pitch pressure-testing, mock investor sessions with written feedback, and Q&A preparation.

What to expect

The practical details

Who you work withThe same small team from the first workshop to the final delivery.
TimelinesFrom four weeks for a Story Sprint to three or four months for Series A Readiness.
RevisionsAgreed rounds of revision on the narrative and deck, set out before we start.
PaymentStaged across the project: at kick-off, midway or monthly, and on delivery, depending on the package.
After launchOngoing support from a three-month minimum, for content, updates and design.
LocationStudios in London and Milan. Working with founders in person and remotely.
